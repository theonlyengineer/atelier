          Getting started

          1. Open the page you want to automate, and sign in if it needs it.
          2. Record a workflow, and give it a short name.
          3. Do the task once. Every click and every field is captured.
          4. Point at the result with Capture result — before it exists is fine, Atelier waits for it.
          5. Save. Atelier writes the workflow; you confirm it here.

          Atelier only ever gets access to sites you record on, and never records a password.
